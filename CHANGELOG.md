# Changelog

## Unreleased

* Agility goals show estimated laps left after a recognized course lap, with a configurable inactivity timeout.
* Manually collapsed goal cards are no longer promoted by XP, while other active cards can move above them.

## 1.0.3 — 2026-11-19

* Apply external goal and preference changes to the active profile before further tracking updates.
* Reload saved goals and preferences when returning to a profile, while preserving matching session statistics.
* Retain session XP and logged-in time for each profile until RuneLite closes or restarts.
* Manually collapsed goal cards stay in place when training activity reorders other cards.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-11-18

* The dashboard explains how to add a goal when the current profile has none.
* Pending goal actions are canceled when switching characters or game modes.
* Saving a goal fills in an unknown Created date; changing its target updates the date.

## 1.0.1— 2026-11-16

* Manually collapsed goal cards stay closed until expanded, edited, or completed.
* Recently trained skills move to the top of the goal list when a new training activity starts.
* Hover over a goal's progress bar to see XP gained and the total XP needed for the goal.
* Expanded goal cards show when the goal was created. Older goals show an unknown creation time.
